## Method and scope

The register contains 74 unique source documents across four official repositories. File digests were retained in the research workspace for collection integrity. Overview duplicates are not additional independent corroboration.

The findings distinguish documented behavior, proposal, local validation, and unresolved remote integration. All architecture diagrams, scope choices, and acceptance criteria are Dagflare proposals. Pricing and limits are dated evidence; recheck them before purchasing, provisioning, or promising service levels.

No live Cloudflare account resources were created as part of this research. No customer data or secret values were collected.
